HOW TO PLAY

BETA · PLAYER 2
←↑↓→ move  ENTER bomb

Online guests (BETA, GAMMA, DELTA): arrow keys or the touch pad.
HEALTH
Everyone starts with 100 HP shown above your fighter and in the top bar. Bombs deal 35 (3 hits knocks you out), mega bombs and big chains deal a critical 40–50, abilities 15–25, lava and meteors 30, monsters 20 (bosses 30). A hit gives up to 2s of safety. ❤️ heals 35 HP (up to 150).
POWER-UPS
🔥 range  💣 extra bomb  ⚡ speed
🛡️ shield  👟 ghost boots (walk through crates)
🧨 mega bomb  ⏱️ remote bombs  ❤️ heal
💪 bomb throw  ❄️ freeze  🔄 swap places
Power-ups are rarer now: only a few can be on the field at once.
MONSTERS & BOSSES
Slime, Fire Imp, Ice Monster (slows you), Ghost, Robot Guard (fires missiles), Shadow Beast (vanishes). Bosses (Lava King, Frost Titan, Shadow Lord, Mecha Overlord) show a health bar and flash warning tiles before every attack.
RULES
Last fighter standing wins the round. Walk into a bomb to kick it. Bombs chain. Walls stop flames, crates burn and may drop power-ups. Spawn points are random every round. 1 player gets a smaller arena, 2 players a medium one, and 3–4 player online battles a large one.
