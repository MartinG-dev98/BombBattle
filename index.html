HEALTH
Everyone starts with 100 HP shown above your fighter and in the top bar. Bombs deal 35 (3 hits knocks you out), mega bombs and big chains deal a critical 40–50, abilities 15–25, lava and meteors 30, monsters 20 (bosses 30). A hit gives up to 2s of safety. ❤️ heals 35 HP (up to 150).
POWER-UPS
🔥 range  💣 extra bomb  ⚡ speed
🛡️ shield  👟 ghost boots (walk through crates)
🧨 mega bomb  ⏱️ remote bombs  ❤️ heal
💪 bomb throw  ❄️ freeze  🔄 swap places
Power-ups are rarer now: only a few can be on the field at once.
MONSTERS & BOSSES
Slime, Fire Imp, Ice Monster (slows you), Ghost, Robot Guard (fires missiles), Shadow Beast (vanishes). Every map has its own monsters, waves and boss: Lava King, Frost Titan, Shadow Lord or Mecha Overlord. Bosses show a health bar and flash warning tiles before every attack.
RULES
Last fighter standing wins the round. Walk into a bomb to kick it. Bombs chain. Walls stop flames, crates burn and may drop power-ups. Spawn points are random every round. 1 player gets a smaller arena, 2 players a medium one, and 3–4 player online battles a large one.
ESC pause (in a menu: back)  R restart round  M mute
★ STARS
Stars are paid at the end of a match and depend on how you played. Spend them on Elite fighters and bomb skins.
Battles: win 4 ★ · lose 1 ★ (+1 per round you won, max 2) · +1 per rival you knock out (max 3) · +1 per 100 damage dealt (max 2) · +1 per 5 monsters (max 2).
Co-op Siege: win 6 ★ · Boss Rush: win 10 ★ · lose 1 ★ · +1 if you are still standing · +1 per 5 monsters (max 3).
Solo Survival: 1 ★ + 1 per full minute survived (max 6).
No stars for matches under 45 seconds or if you did nothing. Crates and chains give no stars. Max 15 ★ per match. After 50 ★ in one day, rewards are halved until tomorrow. Online, every player earns their own stars on their own device.
